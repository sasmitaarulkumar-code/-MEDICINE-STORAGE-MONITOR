The Challenge

Why Conventional Temperature Logging Fails

Hospitals, community pharmacies, and rural clinics across the world still rely heavily on manual clipboard checks twice a day. When a refrigerator door fails or a compressor trips overnight, deviations are only discovered after critical vaccines and insulin have suffered irreversible thermal degradation.

Delayed Problem Detection

Manual checks occur at 9 AM and 5 PM. A cooling failure at 6 PM remains completely unnoticed for 15 hours until the next morning shift.

Simplistic Threshold Alerts

Existing digital monitors only beep after temperature reaches 8.1°C. They do not analyze trends, leaving zero reaction time for staff.

No Inventory Correlation

Raw sensor logs lack batch tracking. Pharmacists cannot instantly determine which medicines, batches, or patient doses were impacted.

Product Innovation

10 Questions MEDGUARD Solves Intelligently

Rather than a trivial threshold tripwire, MEDGUARD acts as an operational intelligence co-pilot:

1

What is happening?

Multi-sensor telemetry: Temperature, humidity, light intrusion, door latch state, and primary power.

2

Why is it happening?

Identifies root causes: Door left open, compressor loss, mains power outage, or sudden light intrusion.

3

How serious is it?

0–100 Weighted Storage Risk Score (Safe, Caution, High Risk, Critical) evaluating magnitude and persistence.

4

Which medicines are affected?

Direct linkage to medicine batch inventory, showing affected quantities, expiry dates, and manufacturer guidelines.

5

How much time is available to respond?

Predictive trend slope estimates minutes until threshold breach while the unit is still within safe limits.

6

What should the user do?

Standardized cold-chain SOP guidance: Quarantine instructions, inspection prompts, and escalation paths.
